2. Opening the site

The site runs on Google Cloud Run (Google Cloud EMEA Ltd., region europe-west1, Belgium). When your browser requests a page, Google's infrastructure receives your IP address, the time, the requested address and your browser's user agent, and writes them to the service's request log. That log is kept for security and for finding faults, and Google Cloud Logging deletes it after 30 days.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (running the site securely).

3. The session cookie (always on)

On your first visit the server sets one cookie, stf_guest: a random identifier with a signature, readable only by the server (HttpOnly). It keeps your copilot conversations, inspection photos and the state of your twin separate from everyone else's. It holds no name, email or address and expires after 30 days without a visit. You can delete it, together with everything kept under it, with the button below.
Legal basis: § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (strictly necessary for the service you request) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Your browser also stores a few settings locally (light or dark theme, which panels are pinned, your consent choice). They never leave your browser.

4. Usage measurement (only if you click “Accept”)

The banner on your first visit asks whether usage may be measured. Only if you accept, the server stores one row per page you open:

  • the session identifier from section 3,
  • the page and the time,
  • your IP address shortened to its network: the last part is set to zero (for example 93.184.216.0), so it no longer points to one connection. The full address is not stored, and no location is looked up from it,
  • your browser's user agent (the browser and operating system it reports).

The rows are stored in the database described in section 8 and deleted after 90 days, or straight away with the “Delete my data” button in section 3. With your consent, the operator also gets one short email per session, sent through Google's Gmail servers, naming the session identifier, the page and your browser. It contains no address and no location. Your choice is remembered for 180 days in the stf_consent cookie and in local storage. You can withdraw it at any time by clearing this site's cookies and site data; the banner then asks again. Withdrawing does not affect what was processed before.
Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG.

5. The AI copilot

The copilot is an AI system. Its answers are written by a language model from the twin's own data, the project documents and, for some questions, a web search. They can be wrong. Do not rely on them for real machines, safety decisions or anything personal.

To answer, the server sends your question and the twin's data needed for it to one of these model providers. It tries them in this order and moves to the next one if a provider is unavailable. If none is available, the twin's own tools answer without any language model.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the answer you ask for) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Voice

If you use the microphone or “read aloud”, the recording of your question (for transcription) or the answer's text (for speech) is sent to ElevenLabs, Inc. (USA), or, if it is unavailable, to Groq or Google. The audio is not stored by this site.

6. Inspection photos

The twin renders photos of its own virtual cookies and saves them to your session. Only you can see them. They are deleted with the “Delete my data” button, and otherwise removed oldest first once the store holds 2,000 photos. Please upload nothing but the twin's own photos.

7. Error logs

Server warnings and errors are stored in the database below for finding faults and deleted after 90 days. They are not meant to contain IP addresses or conversation text.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
