About this site

A personal, non-commercial portfolio project: a digital twin of a simulated fischertechnik “Factory Simulation 24V” training model with an AI copilot. It is not connected to real machines, is not a certified safety or security product, and its content, including the copilot's AI-written answers, may contain errors. References to standards (such as IEC 62443 or ISO 13849) describe what was modelled, not a certification.
