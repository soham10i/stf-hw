Soham's stated goal for the Smart Tabletop Factory work (2026-09-06) is to
**demonstrate specific problem-solving publicly** for better job or business
opportunities — not to ship a general product.

Consequence for how to help: prefer depth on one hard, nameable problem over
breadth. Reject "rebuild a Siemens-style digital twin platform" framings — a
worse clone of a six-figure product is not a differentiator. The narrow problem
this project actually owns is [[stf-single-source-thesis]].
